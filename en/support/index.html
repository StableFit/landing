Welcome! We are here to help you get the most out of StableFit - the app for tracking progress, transformation photos, and working with your coach.

How does StableFit work?

StableFit is a mobile app that allows you to:

  • Record body measurements and track changes
  • Store progress photos and compare results
  • Receive forms and instructions from your coach
  • Receive push notifications about important updates
i

Registration is available only via a personal invite link from your coach. If you have not received a link yet, contact your coach or write to us.
