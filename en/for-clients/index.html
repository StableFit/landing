Key features

Everything for effective training of your clients

StableFit combines the features of several apps in one place, simplifying your whole training flow and helping you reach results faster.
Training according to individual programs, logging of results, and a clear process without extra effort.
  • Personalized plans for every client
  • Simple and clear exercise execution
  • Ability to add exercise videos and notes to workouts
Exercise video recording, feedback, and training adjustments for faster and safer client progress.
  • Difficulty ratings for exercises
  • Training videos
  • Program adjustments in real time

  • Easy logging with AI
