Coaches don't play. And they don't rest. They burn out

Blog · 21 September 2026

But another question comes up: where does it even come from?

And the answer is not always that you "just work too much."

The deeper cause of burnout is inner tension. Constant work can be only one of the ways you deal with it.

Here is how that can look.

1. "I have to"

  • "I need to work more"
  • "A good coach always has to be available"
  • "I cannot say no to a client"

These beliefs can feel normal. But little by little they turn the job into an endless list of things you have to do.

Even rest starts to bring guilt. For a coach with these beliefs, working to exhaustion can feel completely normal.

2. "If I am not useful - why would anyone need me at all?"

Sometimes a coach starts measuring themselves only by how much they do.

  • More clients.
  • More sessions.
  • More programs.
  • More results.

Because just being "good enough" somehow is not enough.

3. You work so you do not have to be alone with yourself

Work fills the whole day. There are always clients, messages, a program or a new task.

And while you are busy, you do not have to stop and think about what actually bothers you.

4. You chase a goal that never lets you stop

  • "I need to earn even more"
  • "I need to get even more clients"
  • "I need to become more successful"

A big goal on its own is not a problem.

But sometimes it becomes so important that a coach is ready to work at the limit - just so they do not have to stop.

And here is something important to understand.

Burnout does not always mean you simply need to work less.

Sometimes it is worth looking deeper: why can you not stop at all?

  • What makes you stay busy all the time?
  • Why does rest bring guilt?
  • Why does it feel like you just need to work a little more - and then you will finally exhale?
  • Are you actually doing work you want to do?
  • If things used to be fine and now they are not - what changed?

Because if you do not understand where this inner tension comes from, you can just keep coming back to the same point.

And what to do if you already recognized yourself in the signs of burnout - we will unpack in the next piece.
