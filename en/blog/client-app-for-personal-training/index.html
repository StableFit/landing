What a personal trainer's client actually sees in the app

Blog · 21 September 2026

When a client searches for a "fitness app", the store hands them a step counter. They need something else: the place you invited them into, where your program lives.

A personal trainer's client app is not a shelf of video classes. It is the other half of your CRM. You coach. They train. You both look at the same thing.

An invite, not a link to a spreadsheet

Sign-up starts with you. You add the person - they get an invite into their app. They do not need a separate web cabinet.

If they download "something for themselves", you are back to two worlds: your sheet and their notes.

What is on the screen on training day

Today. The exercises. The sets. No Excel screenshot, no waiting for your reply at 10 p.m.

If you coach nutrition, the log sits next to that, not in a third tracker. Measurements and photos live on a card you both see.

Chat next to the data

A question about a knee should land beside the program, not in a thread between memes. You answer in context instead of digging through chat history.

The client app is free. The coach pays after three people. There is no till for the client.

What is not here

No studio group timetable and no one else's brand on top of yours. This is the app for your people, not a gym membership.

No separate "social network for athletes". There is a process: program, fact, progress, one chat.

In short

The client app is not another fitness product. It is the screen you set: the day, the progress, the conversation.

In StableFit the client joins from your invite. You can run up to 3 people for free - enough to see if they open the day without you.
