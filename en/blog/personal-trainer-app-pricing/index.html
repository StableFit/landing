What a personal trainer app costs

Blog · 21 September 2026

Search "what a personal trainer app costs" and you often land on a gym till or a subscription "for everyone who wants to lose weight". You need a different number: what a solo trainer pays to run their own people.

In StableFit you start with no card. Up to three clients free. Then a subscription in the store where Pro lives.

The free start is three live cards

Three clients is enough to test the process, not to believe a store screenshot. Client card, program on their phone, progress, chat.

If three people already mean Excel chaos, the problem is not the plan. It is the tool.

$20 a month or $100 a year

Past three people: $20 a month or $100 a year. Billed through the App Store or Google Play, in local currency.

Yearly is the better deal once you know the process lives in the app, not in chat.

Who pays

The client downloads their app for free. There is no till for them. You pay for Pro.

Cancel in the store where the subscription was bought. Refunds follow Apple or Google rules. There is no separate StableFit checkout.

What the price does not include

No group slot booking, gym till, or white label. Finance and memberships for your own clients are in the works - still one trainer, not a studio.

If you want a plan "for 12 coaches and a front desk", that is a different product. This price is for a solo practice.

In short

A personal trainer app costs zero while you have three clients. Then $20 or $100, and only the coach pays.

Test it on three with no card. If the client opens the day themselves, the price is no longer theory.
