It happened: StableFit is now a full coach assistant

Blog · 21 September 2026

New chat, more stats and a subscription. Look for your promo code with 50% off the first payment already in the app.

Chat

Audio and video messages, photo editing, pinning, replies and reactions - talking with a client is now as easy as chatting with friends.

Exercise stats

We added stats in the Workouts section: a training calendar, tonnage by muscle group and working-weight trends. Open any exercise card and you will see the full history: working weight, reps and tonnage.

A client's progress shows up in the details, not just in the numbers.

Subscription

For clients the app stays free - only the coach pays. You can work with the first 3 clients at no charge, then the subscription lifts every limit.
