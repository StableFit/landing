If the program lives in one app and the plate in another, the client is living two lives again. You see the sets and miss whether protein is even holding.

Nutrition for a personal trainer's clients is not a separate diet office for a gym. It is part of coaching: a target, a log, macros, your note.

## Why a third tracker breaks the work

They forget the password to "that food app". They screenshot the plate into Telegram. You reconcile it by hand at night.

There is no link to the program and the measurements. A deficit on paper, lifts dropping - and you argue which number is fresh.

## What should sit in one place

Macro targets. Meal logs. A photo or a barcode if that is faster. You see what the client sees, with no extra login.

Chat sits next to it: "swap the grain" lands beside the entry, not in a thread between memes.

## Not a gym till and not a counter "for everyone"

Do not look for a studio white label or a buffet menu for groups. You need a solo trainer and their people.

Do not look for another public calorie counter. Look for a place you invite the client into together with the program.

## How to switch it on without drama

Start with the people whose food you already coach in chat. One agreement: the log lives in the app. A plate screenshot is a backup.

You do not need a perfect diary from day one. You need to see the week, not a mood.

## In short

A trainer's client nutrition should live where the program and progress live. Otherwise it is a third app again.
