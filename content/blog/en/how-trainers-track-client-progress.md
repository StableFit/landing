"I feel better" is not progress. Progress is a number, a photo, and a completion fact you can open in a month without hunting the camera roll.

A personal trainer does not need a private diary. They need a place the client sees too. Otherwise the session turns into whose number is fresher.

## What to collect so it is coaching, not a hobby

Weight and measurements - on a rhythm, not "when we remember". Before/after photos from the same angle. Working loads and missed sessions. If you coach food, macros sit next to that, not in another app.

A note about how they feel needs a home too. Otherwise it drops into Telegram between a meme and a reschedule.

## Why a sheet and a chat break this

Excel archives a row. It will not remind the client to measure. Photos do not live in a sheet. The client does not see their row.

In chat, progress photos drown. Search finds "weight" on gym invoices. There is no six-month chart. There is a vibe.

## Progress the client sees themselves

When they open the chart and the photos, you do not sell the point of the work every week. A dip in motivation is held by the picture, not your voice in a messenger.

Measurement reminders take you out of "please send the numbers". You are the coach, not the archive clerk.

## How to start without a perfect system

Three clients. One agreement: measurements and photos live in the app. Chat is for questions, not storage.

Do not backfill five years. Start today. Old Excel files can stay as a report, not the workplace.

## In short

Tracking client progress is not keeping a spreadsheet. It is giving the person the same trend you see - and not proving it in words every week.
