StableFit PRO is now on Censuus. It is a census of apps: rank comes from real visits, not from an editor's pick.

## Where to find us

The card is called StableFit PRO: for trainers. The category is Health & Fitness. From there you open the App Store, Google Play and the website.

[Open the StableFit card on Censuus](https://censuus.com/a/stablefit-pro-00c5)

## What this catalog is

Censuus keeps a live ranking by visit traffic. Free apps rise on real visits. A place on the list is not invented there.

## Why it matters for a coach

If you are looking for a tool to work with clients online and offline, StableFit is now visible outside the stores too. The card says it plainly: this is for fitness trainers, not a step counter and not a gym till.

For clients the app stays free. The coach pays.
