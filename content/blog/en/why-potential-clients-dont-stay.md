They read your posts, maybe even follow you, show up for the first session - and that is it.

Why?

Because you offer what you know how to do, not what they want to get right now.

## This is called relevance

The client wants A - you offer A. Not B and not C.

A woman wants to lose a bit of weight, tone up, and train at home.

And you offer her in-gym personal training to build muscle mass.

Miss.

She literally told you what she needs. And you offered something else.

## Get more specific than "for everyone"

So instead of "personal training for everyone", try being more specific:

Home workouts for women who need to get back in shape fast.

Or:

A 12-week program for men 35+ who want to lose the belly without 6 sessions a week.

## So the client sees the solution themselves

And then a simple thought should show up: "Oh, this is about me."

That is when you do not have to explain why they need your service.

They already see their solution in it.
